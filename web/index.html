What this snapshot measures · dates and coverage

Counts cover direct top-level entries explicitly tagged with the selected raw language code. Translation mentions, interwiki links, and nested form lists do not create votes. Duplicate senses, parts of speech and etymologies share one edition vote. Explicit form entries count.

Identity preserves case, accents, combining marks, spacing and script. No Unicode normalization or language-code merging is applied. Equal counts share competition rank (1, 1, 3). Alphabetical display order uses the selected language’s collation where supported, then exact Unicode order for otherwise equal forms. Score ties remain alphabetical in either direction. Frozen downloads retain their original deterministic order. Rank is always within the full selected language, including when search filters the view.

“Not observed” means no matching direct entry in a successfully processed extraction. It is not a claim about all Wiktionaries or all words. Failed and unprocessed sources remain unknown. Missing identity fields fail processing; unscoped redirects and explicit “unknown” language tags are excluded and reported.

The separately named foreign_only_count in downloads excludes the edition with the identical language code. Simple English stays an independent edition, matching the earlier EN comparison. Codes and macrolanguages are never silently equated.
